JEVVIESCOUT SETTINGS

Connect your scout.

JevvieScout sends extracted post and profile text to your server-side classifier. Your X cookies and session are never included.

For local development, keep the Next.js app running on port 3000. HTTPS endpoints require a one-time browser permission.
Privacy boundary
  • Reads only X content rendered in the current page.
  • Does not access, store, or transmit X authentication cookies.
  • Sends post text, public author identity, and visible profile context to your endpoint.
